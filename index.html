Building now

Ravioli

I’m exploring collaborative AI with Ravioli, where an AI can join your group chat, share context with everyone, and take part in the conversation.

Jino, Ravioli’s first AI, can chat with you privately or join your group. He remembers what happened, hosts games, and weighs in when the group wants another perspective.

Selected work

Making personal data harder to find

Cloaked

Personal information can be scattered across hundreds of data broker sites. Removing it by hand is slow, repetitive work.

At Cloaked, I designed and shipped the Data Removal system to automate discovery and removal across more than 120 data broker sites. It became the company’s flagship product.

I joined early and built backend infrastructure as the engineering team grew from 3 to 15. That work also included spam prevention and protection against fraud, phishing, and impersonation.

Explore Cloaked’s Data Removal

Putting spare computing power to work

Project Santa Cruz at VMware

A router can have computing capacity sitting idle. Our question was whether that capacity could run applications close to where they were needed.

I helped design and build a Kubernetes-on-Router solution to deploy and manage applications on SD-WAN routers. The hackathon project became a funded, full-time team within VMware’s Office of the CTO and was showcased at VMworld 2021.
